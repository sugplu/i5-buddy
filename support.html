Support

Help with I-5 Buddy, and how to reach us.

I-5 Buddy is made by Dominares. We read every message.

Get the app

iPhone: get the I-5 Buddy app, free on the App Store.

Android: I-5 Buddy is coming to Google Play. You can use it today in Chrome: open i5.dominares.org, then the menu > Install app (or Add to Home screen).

Contact us

Write to support@dominares.org, or tap Settings > About > Send feedback in the app. We reply by email.

Try it without driving

Tap Demo drive on the first screen. I-5 Buddy pretends to drive along I-5, so you can hear how it works from the couch. Tap End trip to stop.

On the road

Set it up before you drive. Narration is hands-free; leave the screen to passengers.

  • Tap Start GPS before you pull out, and allow location while using the app.
  • I-5 Buddy covers Interstate 5 in California. Away from I-5 it stays quiet until you're back on the road.
  • Keep the app open on screen during the drive. A car charger helps on long trips.

If location is off

In the iPhone app, open the Settings app, tap Apps, then I-5 Buddy, then Location, and choose While Using the App.

In the Android app or a browser, allow location for i5.dominares.org in the browser's settings (in Chrome: Settings, Site settings, Location), make sure the browser itself may use precise location (your phone's Settings, Apps, Chrome, Permissions), then tap Start GPS again.

If you don't hear anything

Turn up the volume, and check Settings > Narration. If the voice sounds wrong, pick another one in Settings > Narration > Voice.

Live road info

Traffic, weather, chain controls, freeway signs and cameras are off until you turn them on in Settings > Live road info. They need a connection.
