Bakersfield to Sacramento on I-5 via Kettleman City

This guide follows I-5 north from exit 257 near Buttonwillow to exit 519 in Sacramento, through cotton country, the Kettleman Hills, Westlands farm country, Los Banos, Tracy, Stockton and the Delta's edge. Gas, EV chargers, food and lodging are listed from OpenStreetMap as mapped, not live, so check ahead before you count on a stop. Miles here are California exit numbers counting up from the Mexico border.

  4. Exit 284 · Land & water

    From November through March, tule fog can fill this southern end of the valley. Full story

  5. Exit 288 · on your right going north · Land & water

    Kern National Wildlife Refuge

    East of here, Kern National Wildlife Refuge protects 11,249 acres of desert uplands, an old river corridor and marsh on the southern edge of what was once Tulare Lake. Full story

  1. Exit 309 · on your right going north · Land & water

    East of here lies the bed of Tulare Lake, once the largest freshwater lake west of the Mississippi River. Full story

  2. Exit 309 · on your left going north · History

  4. Exit 314 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Kettleman Hills

    The long, low hills along the west side are the Kettleman Hills, about 30 miles long and up to about 1,200 feet high. Full story

  5. Exit 319 · on your left going north · History

    The 1928 Kettleman gusher

    In October 1928, after 19 months of drilling, the Milham Exploration Company's Elliott Number 1 well struck oil more than 7,000 feet under the Kettleman Hills. Full story

Miles 319 to 330Fresno County's West Side

The freeway runs through the West Side farmland of Fresno County, which in 2022 led California counties in the value of its almonds, pistachios, garlic and processing tomatoes.

  1. Exit 319 · on your left going north · Towns

    Avenal, the oat field

    Highway 269 crosses the Kettleman Hills to Avenal, whose name is Spanish for oat field. Full story

  2. Exit 322 · Orchards & farms

    Garlic country

    Fresno County grows most of the garlic in the United States, and west side towns such as Huron picked up the slack as Gilroy, once the country's biggest garlic producer, ran out of room. Full story

  3. Exit 325 · on your right going north · Oddities

    Gates Substation bottleneck

    The sprawling electrical yard east of the freeway is Gates Substation, a key junction on the power lines linking Northern and Southern California. Full story

  4. Exit 325 · on your right going north · Towns

    Huron, harvest town

    East of the freeway is Huron, a farm town of about 6,200 people that swells to more than 15,000 at harvest time, when migrant farmworkers arrive. Full story

  5. Exit 327 · History

    Arroyo Pasajero bridges

    The freeway bridges here cross Los Gatos Creek, once called Arroyo Pasajero, the travelers' creek. Full story

  1. Exit 334 · on your right going north · Good stops

    The Harris Ranch restaurant opened beside the freeway in 1977, aimed at local farmers, and soon became a popular stop for travelers on the busy freeway between Los Angeles and San Francisco. Full story

  2. Exit 334 · on your left going north · History

    Coalinga Oil Field

    The long, low ridge between the freeway and Coalinga is Anticline Ridge, part of the Coalinga Oil Field, one of only six California fields to have produced more than a billion barrels of oil. Full story

  3. Exit 337 · on your left going north · Towns

    Coalinga and the Iron Zoo

    Coalinga began in 1888 as a Southern Pacific coaling stop for steam locomotives. Full story

  4. Exit 337 · on your left going north · History

    At 4:42 on the afternoon of May 2, 1983, a magnitude 6.2 earthquake struck Coalinga. Full story

  5. Exit 337 · on your right going north · Orchards & farms

    Harris Ranch feedlot

    Harris Ranch is California's largest beef producer, and its feedlot sits right beside the freeway. Full story

  6. Exit 353 · Orchards & farms

    The nation's top farm county

  7. Exit 355 · Orchards & farms

  1. Exit 357 · History

  3. Exit 365 · History

    El Camino Viejo

  4. Exit 368 · Land & water

    Southwest of Mendota, east of the freeway, the ground sank about 28 feet between 1925 and 1977 because so much groundwater was pumped from under the fields. Full story

  5. Exit 370 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Panoche Hills

    The low hills west of the freeway are the Panoche Hills, rising east of the higher Diablo Range. Full story

Miles 481 to 498Lodi vineyards and the Delta's edge

Vineyards spread east toward Lodi, while the flat, reclaimed islands of the Delta, held in by levees, stretch off to the west.

  1. Exit 481 · History

    The last piece of I-5

    A 4.6-mile stretch of freeway north of Stockton was the very last piece of Interstate 5 to open.

  2. Exit 485 · on your right going north · Towns

    Lodi, wine and root beer

    Highway 12 leads east to Lodi, a town surrounded by vineyards.

  3. Exit 487 · Orchards & farms

    Delta asparagus

    From about 1900 through the 1970s, asparagus was a signature crop of the Delta west of here.

  4. Exit 490 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Sandhill crane reserve

    A couple of miles west of the freeway, off Woodbridge Road, is the 353-acre Woodbridge Ecological Reserve, also called the Isenberg Sandhill Crane Reserve.

  5. Exit 490 · Land & water

    The Delta, California's Holland

    West of the freeway lies the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta, about 57 reclaimed islands and tracts ringed by 1,100 miles of levees.

  6. Exit 493 · Land & water

    Mokelumne, People of the Fish Net

    The freeway crosses the Mokelumne River here, about half a mile below the spot where the Cosumnes River flows into it.

  7. Exit 498 · on your right going north · Land & water

    Cosumnes River Preserve

    The Cosumnes River Preserve, whose visitor center is just east of the freeway, covers nearly 50,000 acres, including some of the largest remaining wetlands and streamside habitat in the Central Valley.

  8. Exit 498 · on your left going north · History

    Locke, a Chinese river town

    About three miles west of the freeway, on the Sacramento River levee a mile north of Walnut Grove, is Locke, the last of the Sacramento River Chinatowns to be built.

Miles 498 to 510Cosumnes wetlands and Elk Grove

Wetlands of the Cosumnes River Preserve lie near the freeway, which runs along the eastern edge of the Stone Lakes refuge as it passes through Elk Grove.

  1. Exit 504 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Stone Lakes National Wildlife Refuge

    The freeway runs along the eastern edge of Stone Lakes National Wildlife Refuge, set aside in 1994.

  2. Exit 506 · on your right going north · Towns

    Elk Grove

    Elk Grove Boulevard leads east into Elk Grove, which began in 1850 as a stagecoach stop for travelers between Sacramento and the Bay Area, when James Hall opened the Elk Grove Hotel.

  3. Exit 510 · on your left going north · Towns

    Freeport, a town born of taxes

    The tiny river town of Freeport, just west of the freeway, began as a way around Sacramento's port taxes.

Questions drivers ask

Where are the rest areas on I-5 between Bakersfield and Sacramento?

The data lists Buttonwillow Rest Area at mile 259.2, Coalinga-Avenal Rest Area at mile 320.2, John "Chuck" Erreca Rest Area at mile 385.9 and Westley Rest Area at mile 444.9, all serving both directions. Dos Amigos Vista Point is also listed at mile 390.0, with no direction stated.

Where can I find gas, EV charging, food and lodging at Kettleman City on I-5?

At exit 309 (CA 41, Kettleman City), the data lists 9 gas stations, Tesla Supercharger and Electrify America chargers, 16 food places and 2 lodging places including Best Western and Quality Inn. This is mapped data, not live.

Which I-5 exits between Bakersfield and Sacramento have the most lodging listed?

Exit 407 (CA 33, Santa Nella) lists 8 lodging places, including Best Western and Holiday Inn Express. Exit 476 (March Lane) in Stockton lists 7, including Hilton and Courtyard.

What exit is Lost Hills on I-5?

Lost Hills is at exit 278 (CA 46). The data lists 14 gas stations there, Electrify America and Tesla chargers, 11 food places and 2 lodging places, as mapped. Going north, Blackwells Corner is also at this exit, on the left.

What can I see along I-5 near Stockton?

I-5 runs through Stockton past the deepwater port and crosses the Calaveras River. Highway 4 heads east at exit 472 on the Crosstown Freeway, built in the early 1970s through part of Little Manila.
