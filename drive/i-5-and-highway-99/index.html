I-5 and Highway 99 in California

Before I-5, US 99 was the main West Coast highway, created in 1926 and running from the Mexican border to the Canadian border. This guide follows the places along I-5 where Highway 99 still shows up, from the truck lanes at exit 162 to Old Highway 99 near exit 755. I-5 Buddy tells these stories out loud as you drive.

The Highway 99 stories on I-5

  1. Exit 162 · History

    Newhall Pass Interchange

    The separate truck lanes that loop around the main lanes are the original four-lane freeway, built as US 99. Full story

  2. Exit 191 · History

    Highway 99 under the lake

    In 1933 the three-lane Ridge Route Alternate, later US 99, opened through the canyon of Piru Creek, with a middle lane that drivers in both directions used for passing, nicknamed the suicide lane. US 99 was called the Main Street of California, and in the 1930s Dust Bowl migrant farm workers traveled it across the state. Full story

  3. Exit 221 · History

    Start of the West Side Freeway

    Highway 99 and I-5 meet at the Wheeler Ridge interchange. Local groups pushed for a west side freeway as early as 1945, and when the Interstate system was created in 1956, it won out over turning Highway 99 into the Interstate. Full story

  4. Exit 225 · History

    James Dean's speeding ticket

    On September 30, 1955, the actor James Dean was driving north on Highway 99, on his way to a sports car race in Salinas, when a Highway Patrol officer pulled him over near here and wrote him a speeding ticket. Full story

  5. Exit 355 · Orchards & farms

    The farm water signs

    In June 2011 the Associated Press reported several hundred new signs along I-5 and Highway 99, with lines like Man-made drought and Food grows where water flows. Full story

  6. Exit 481 · History

    The last piece of I-5

    Traffic on the West Side Freeway, the stretch of I-5 down the west side of the San Joaquin Valley, picked up once its second phase opened in 1972, because in Stockton the new freeway came within 4 miles of the Golden State Highway, Route 99.

  7. Exit 633 · Good stops

    Helmick rest area

    On February 27, 1989, while driving to speak at a Rotary Club luncheon in Oroville, he was killed when his car crashed into a Caltrans truck parked on the shoulder of Highway 99. Full story

  8. Exit 688 · Oddities

    The drowned highway

    Before the lake, old Highway 99 ran through Mountain Gate and then plunged into the valley that Shasta Lake now fills. Full story

  10. Exit 755 · History

    Old Highway 99

    A two-lane county road called Old Highway 99 runs between Weed and Yreka, still in the same form it had when it was U.S. 99, the main West Coast highway before I-5. U.S. 99 was created in 1926 and ran from the Mexican border to the Canadian border. Full story

  11. Exit 770 · History

    State of Jefferson

    On November 27, 1941, young men waving hunting rifles stopped traffic on U.S. 99 south of Yreka and handed out a proclamation of independence for the State of Jefferson. Full story

Questions drivers ask

What was Highway 99 in California?

US 99 was created in 1926 and ran from the Mexican border to the Canadian border, and it was the main West Coast highway before I-5. It was called the Main Street of California.

Where do I-5 and Highway 99 meet?

They meet at the Wheeler Ridge interchange, at exit 221. Local groups pushed for a west side freeway as early as 1945, and when the Interstate system was created in 1956, it won out over turning Highway 99 into the Interstate.

Where can I still see part of the old US 99 along I-5?

At exit 162, the separate truck lanes that loop around the main lanes at the Newhall Pass Interchange are the original four-lane freeway, built as US 99. Near exit 755, a two-lane county road called Old Highway 99 runs between Weed and Yreka in the same form it had when it was US 99.

What happened to old Highway 99 near Shasta Lake?

Before the lake, old Highway 99 ran through Mountain Gate and then plunged into the valley that Shasta Lake now fills. The landmark at exit 688 is called The drowned highway.

Who got stopped for speeding on Highway 99 near I-5?

On September 30, 1955, a Highway Patrol officer pulled over the actor James Dean on Highway 99 near exit 225 and wrote him a speeding ticket. He was driving north, on his way to a sports car race in Salinas.
