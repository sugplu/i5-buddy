Sacramento to Stockton on I-5

This stretch of I-5 runs from exit 473 at Stockton to exit 519 in Sacramento, passing the Port of Stockton, the Lodi vineyards, the Delta levees and the Cosumnes River Preserve. I-5 Buddy talks you through what you pass, exit by exit. Gas, EV, food and lodging are listed from OpenStreetMap as mapped, not live.

Miles 473 to 481Stockton

I-5 runs through Stockton, past the deepwater port and its channel to San Francisco Bay, and crosses the Calaveras River.

  1. Exit 473 · on your left going north · History

    Port of Stockton

    The docks just west of the freeway belong to the Port of Stockton, a seaport more than 70 nautical miles from the ocean.

  2. Exit 474 · on your right going north · History

    University of the Pacific

    About a mile and a half east of the freeway is the University of the Pacific.

  3. Exit 476 · Land & water

    Calaveras River, river of skulls

    The freeway crosses the Calaveras River here.

  4. Exit 477 · History

    Benjamin Holt and the Caterpillar

    The Benjamin Holt Drive exit shares its name with a Stockton inventor.

Miles 481 to 498Lodi vineyards and the Delta's edge

Vineyards spread east toward Lodi, while the flat, reclaimed islands of the Delta, held in by levees, stretch off to the west.

  1. Exit 481 · History

    The last piece of I-5

    A 4.6-mile stretch of freeway north of Stockton was the very last piece of Interstate 5 to open.

  2. Exit 485 · on your right going north · Towns

    Lodi, wine and root beer

    Highway 12 leads east to Lodi, a town surrounded by vineyards.

  3. Exit 487 · Orchards & farms

    Delta asparagus

    From about 1900 through the 1970s, asparagus was a signature crop of the Delta west of here.

  4. Exit 490 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Sandhill crane reserve

    A couple of miles west of the freeway, off Woodbridge Road, is the 353-acre Woodbridge Ecological Reserve, also called the Isenberg Sandhill Crane Reserve.

  5. Exit 490 · Land & water

    The Delta, California's Holland

    West of the freeway lies the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta, about 57 reclaimed islands and tracts ringed by 1,100 miles of levees.

  6. Exit 493 · Land & water

    Mokelumne, People of the Fish Net

    The freeway crosses the Mokelumne River here, about half a mile below the spot where the Cosumnes River flows into it.

  7. Exit 498 · on your right going north · Land & water

    Cosumnes River Preserve

    The Cosumnes River Preserve, whose visitor center is just east of the freeway, covers nearly 50,000 acres, including some of the largest remaining wetlands and streamside habitat in the Central Valley.

  8. Exit 498 · on your left going north · History

    Locke, a Chinese river town

    About three miles west of the freeway, on the Sacramento River levee a mile north of Walnut Grove, is Locke, the last of the Sacramento River Chinatowns to be built.

Miles 498 to 510Cosumnes wetlands and Elk Grove

Wetlands of the Cosumnes River Preserve lie near the freeway, which runs along the eastern edge of the Stone Lakes refuge as it passes through Elk Grove.

  1. Exit 504 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Stone Lakes National Wildlife Refuge

    The freeway runs along the eastern edge of Stone Lakes National Wildlife Refuge, set aside in 1994.

  2. Exit 506 · on your right going north · Towns

    Elk Grove

    Elk Grove Boulevard leads east into Elk Grove, which began in 1850 as a stagecoach stop for travelers between Sacramento and the Bay Area, when James Hall opened the Elk Grove Hotel.

  3. Exit 510 · on your left going north · Towns

    Freeport, a town born of taxes

    The tiny river town of Freeport, just west of the freeway, began as a way around Sacramento's port taxes.

Miles 510 to 518Along the river to Land Park

The freeway runs close to the Sacramento River through the south side of Sacramento, near William Land Park and the zoo.

  1. Exit 516 · on your right going north · Good stops

    Sacramento Zoo and Land Park

    The Sutterville Road exit leads to William Land Park and the Sacramento Zoo, a few blocks east of the freeway.

Miles 518 to 519Downtown Sacramento

I-5 runs between downtown and the river, past Old Sacramento and the Tower Bridge.

  1. Exit 518 · Towns

    Sacramento, the state capital

    Sacramento has been California's capital since 1854.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Lodi on I-5?

Exit 485 is CA 12, where Highway 12 leads east to Lodi, a town surrounded by vineyards. Eight gas stations are mapped at this exit, along with ChargePoint and EVgo charging.

Where are the EV chargers between Sacramento and Stockton on I-5?

EV chargers are mapped at seven exits in this stretch, including exit 476 (Tesla Supercharger), exit 481 (ChargePoint and Electrify America), exit 485 (ChargePoint and EVgo) and exit 510 (Shell, Tesla Supercharger and bp pulse). Exits 512, 515 and 519A also have mapped chargers. This is mapped data, not live availability.

What is the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta that I-5 passes?

West of the freeway near exit 490 lies the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta, about 57 reclaimed islands and tracts ringed by 1,100 miles of levees.

Which exit has the most gas stations on this part of I-5?

Exit 485 at CA 12 has eight gas stations mapped, the most in this stretch. Exit 519A at Q Street has five.
