Checked 2026-11-11 by the adversarial fact-checker. I read every src and src2 myself with wiki.py full or WebFetch, and ran where.py on every coordinate. lint_content.py reports 0 errors and 0 warnings.

**Totals:** 39 places checked. 34 remain. 5 were removed: 3 merged into neighbors (Trestles, Swallows of Capistrano, Oldest McDonald's), 1 merged into a neighbor's "more" (Zanja Madre), and 1 dropped (Philippe the Original). All 5 removals were for CROWDING; no place failed on facts. Of the 34 remaining, 27 changed (including the merge targets) and 7 were left as they were (Dana Point, Trabuco Creek, El Toro Y, Santa Ana River, La Mirada, Santa Fe Springs, Sears tower). 12 trivia checked: 2 reworded (Tustin "in 1942", the McDonald's "neon" sign), 0 deleted, and every answer index is correct. 8 zones checked: they run 70 to 76 to 86 to 95 to 103 to 112 to 124 to 134 to 140 with no gaps or overlaps. Two zones changed: the 103-112 fall note and the 124-134 text.

After the merges, no 2-mile window holds four or more places (where.py placement, using the exit for points more than 4 miles off). The tightest spots are three places in 1.7 miles at exits 70 to 72 and at 82 to 84.
