# ca1 verification log (California exits 0 to 70, San Diego County)

Fact-checked 2026-11-11 by the adversarial verifier. The researcher's notes were lost, so every claim in all 35 places, 6 zones and 12 trivia questions was checked again against sources read in this session. Every place's coordinates were run through `tools/where.py`, and the exit was compared with the reported exit-mile.

Result: 35 places checked, 0 deleted, 27 changed (3 of them had their exit moved). All 6 zones checked (2 changed). All 12 trivia questions checked (3 changed). Zones run 0 to 70 with no gaps or overlaps, and ca2 picks up at 70. No place, zone or trivia question repeats ca2.json, which starts at exit 71, or ca-gaps.json, which has nothing below exit 104.
