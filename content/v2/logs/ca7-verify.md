# ca7 verification log (CA exits 560-680)

Adversarial fact-check of `content/v2/ca7.json`, 2026-11-11.

- Checked: 55 places, 8 zones, 12 trivia questions.
- Places: 44 fixed, 5 confirmed unchanged, 6 deleted. All 6 deletions were crowding merges into a neighbor's text or "more". No place failed at its core. The file now has 49 places.
- Zones: all 8 changed (text, seasons, or both). Trivia: 3 fixed, 0 deleted.
- Every Wikipedia source was downloaded again in this session (`wiki.py full`). Non-Wikipedia pages were read with WebFetch. The Colusa 2024 crop report, the Tehama 2024 crop report, the CDFA walnut paper and the Ag in the Classroom prune fact sheet were downloaded as PDFs and read as extracted text.
- Coordinates: every place with coordinates was compared with Wikipedia's coordinates (or OpenStreetMap/Nominatim where Wikipedia has none or is wrong) and run through `where.py`. Every exit is within 2 of the where.py exit-mile, except three far-off places whose exit is the signed access exit: Sites Reservoir (586, Maxwell; Maxwell Sites Road begins at that interchange), Rancho Larkin's Children (595, Norman Road / Princeton) and Gerber (642, Proberta / Gerber; 3.4 mi off, so the app places it by its coordinates anyway).
- Writing rules: a script checked word limits, sentence counts, direction words, dashes, hype words and exclamation marks. `lint_content.py` reports 0 errors and 0 warnings.
- Crowding: before this pass there were five clusters with four or more places within about 2 miles (Maxwell 586-588, Corning 630-632, Red Bluff 647-651 with nine places, Cottonwood 662-665, Redding 678-679). After the merges and moves below, no 2-mile window holds more than three places, including across the ca6 and ca8 seams.
- Zones: 560-578-597-619-636-649-662-675-680. No gaps or overlaps.

## Researcher's open items, settled

1. **Visibility of Mount Shasta (657) and Lassen Peak.** Kept Shasta visible. Wikipedia Mount Shasta says it "can be seen from the floor of the Central Valley 140 miles to the south" on a clear winter day, and exit 657 is about 77 miles away. Lassen visibility is supported by the NPS photo caption "The May 22, 1915 eruption of Lassen Peak as seen from Red Bluff, California". Lassen now sits at exit 668, which is about 41 miles from the peak, so it is closer than Red Bluff (about 44 miles). See Lassen Peak below.
2. **Access exits.** Vina/Lassen's rancho at 630 is within 2 of where.py (628.6). Coleman at 665 is too (664.9). Sites Reservoir at 586 is confirmed: Nominatim puts the start of Maxwell Sites Road at Maxwell beside exit 586. The Reading adobe place was merged into Cottonwood, so its exit no longer matters. The 1846 massacre stays null at 675. Wikipedia now gives the article coordinates (40.585, -122.374), but that is just central Redding, and the text only places the attack at "Reading's Ranch (near present-day Redding)". Reading's ranch ran 19 miles along the river, so the exact spot is not known.
3. **Blocked pages.** I did not use archived copies. Every claim that rested only on a blocked page was re-sourced or removed:
   - The abc10 Granzella's story is blocked (403). The taxidermy and Joe Vaiano claims were removed. The place is now sourced to granzellas.com and flavortownusa.com.
   - The abc10 queen bee story is blocked. The "200,000 queens a year" figure and the queen-shipping details were removed. They were replaced with the Honeybee Discovery Center's own claim, worded as a claim, and the queen-rearing steps from Wikipedia "Queen bee".
   - californiaprunes.org is blocked. The prune place now uses the California Foundation for Agriculture in the Classroom prune fact sheet (cdn.agclassroom.org, "compiled by the California Prune Board") and Wikipedia Pellier Park. As a result, "99 percent" of US prunes became "more than 90 percent", and "about 46,000 acres" was removed.
   - hmdb.org is blocked. The Herbert S. Miles facts were confirmed on waymarking.com instead. The "Redding Rotary and friends" plaque detail could not be confirmed, so it was removed.
4. **Mrs. John Brown coordinates.** Wikipedia's coordinates are wrong: they point to the town of Tehama. Nominatim returns an address-interpolation line for 135 Main St, between 40.1698 and 40.1724. Wikipedia says the house was moved "a little south off the corner" of Main and Willow streets, and OSM puts that corner at about 40.1724, -122.2307. So the point is now 40.1722, -122.2307, about 0.2 mi from the researcher's point. The exit-mile is still 648.4.
5. **Herbert S. Miles.** See that place below.
6. **The researcher's earlier fixes** (College City dates, Delevan counts, Round-Up, Tehama, walnuts and so on) were each checked again against the sources and hold.
7. **Duplicates and the ca6/ca8 seams.** No duplicate places remain. The Sutter Buttes "I spy" prompt was rewritten so it no longer echoes ca6's prompt at exit 553.
