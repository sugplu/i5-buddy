# ca6 verification log (CA exits 460-560)

Adversarial fact-check of `content/v2/ca6.json`, 2026-11-11.

- Checked: 49 places, 10 zones, 12 trivia questions.
- Places: 38 fixed, 5 unchanged, 6 deleted. The 6 deletions are the Sacramento riverfront cluster cut. Their best details were folded into the 4 places that remain there. 43 places remain.
- Trivia: 2 fixed, 0 deleted. Zones: 7 fixed, and they still run 460-467-481-498-510-518-524-531-537-548-560 with no gaps or overlaps.
- Every Wikipedia source was downloaded fresh in this session (`wiki.py full`). Non-Wikipedia sources were read with WebFetch. The water board PDF (r5-2018-0051) was downloaded and its text extracted locally. OpenStreetMap (Overpass) was used to check coordinates for bridges, the crane reserve, the Cosumnes visitor center, the cannery and the Elkhorn Causeway.
- Coordinates: every place with coordinates was compared with Wikipedia `coords` or OpenStreetMap, then run through `where.py`. All exits are within 2 of the where.py exit-mile, with four exceptions. Knights Landing (6.9 mi off, exit 536 is its signed exit), Capay Valley (10.7 mi off, exit 541 is signed for CA 16 and Esparto) and the Sutter Buttes (a mountain 17 mi off) keep their access exits. The Mokelumne crossing sits at exit-mile 495.5, exactly 2.5 from both neighboring exits (493 and 498), so no exit can be within 2; it keeps 493.
- Writing rules: a script checked every text, more, spot, zone and trivia string for word limits, sentence counts, direction words, dashes, hype words and exclamation marks. `tools/lint_content.py` reports 0 errors and 0 warnings.
- Crowding: positions were computed the way build.py places them (coordinates within 4 mi, otherwise the exit's OSM point). The only 2-mile window with 4 or more places is the Sacramento riverfront (Tower Bridge, Boat Section, Railroad Museum, American River at 519.3-520.3), which the brief allows at 4 or 5. Three tighter groups of three remain, below the threshold: Mossdale and Manteca (both at 460.8), with Lathrop at 462.4; Mokelumne, Cosumnes and Locke (495.5-496.3); and Capay, Cache Creek and Yolo (540.8-541.6).
