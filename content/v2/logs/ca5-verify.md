# ca5 verification log (CA exits 330-460)

Adversarial fact-check of `content/v2/ca5.json`, 2026-11-11.

- Checked: 59 places, 9 zones, 12 trivia questions.
- Places: 50 fixed (2 of them renamed), 4 deleted (NAS Lemoore, The Iron Zoo, A giant water battery, O'Neill Forebay), 5 unchanged. Two of the deletions were merged into neighbors (Iron Zoo into Coalinga, the water battery into San Luis Reservoir) to relieve crowding. The file now has 55 places.
- Zones: all 9 edited. Three unsourced season notes were deleted or replaced. Coverage is 330 to 460 with no gaps.
- Trivia: 5 fixed, 0 deleted, 1 added (13 total).
- Biggest factual fixes: the Harris Ranch feedlot's location (exit 337, not Highway 198); the outdated millipede leg record; the contested "only freeway over the Diablo Range" claim and the self-contradicting "Pacheco/Polonio detour" claim; the Iron Zoo's misattributed quote; the defendants in the Westley tire fire suit; the rest area's reopening and cost (Caltrans, $30M); and an unsourced claim that the San Luis refuge "helped bring back" tule elk.

## Method

- Every Wikipedia source was downloaded fresh in this session with the project's wiki.py call wrapper, one request at a time, with plain text, wikitext and coordinates. Infobox values (runway length, dam specs, magnitudes, quake coordinates) were read from the wikitext.
- Non-Wikipedia sources were read with WebFetch: harrisranch.com, weburbanist pages 1 to 3, wwd.ca.gov (2 pages), ohp.parks.ca.gov 344 and 550, usgs.gov, concretepavements.org, gribblenation, fws.gov (San Luis, San Joaquin River), usbr.gov, westsideconnect.com, oag.ca.gov, tracymuseum.org, two Caltrans District 10 pages, deseret.com and earthmagazine.org.
- The San Joaquin County 2024 crop report PDF was downloaded and its text extracted locally with macOS PDFKit.
- Blocked or failed: latimes.com (blocked) and journals.ametsoc.org (403). No workaround was attempted.
- WebSearch was used twice: once for the dust storm location, once to find the Caltrans rest area releases.
- Coordinates were compared with Wikipedia (all match except the corrected feedlot) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (Harris Ranch hotel and feedlot). All were run through where.py. Every exit is within 2 of where.py's exit-mile, except far-off places at their access exit (Coalinga, the 1983 quake, Mendota, Firebaugh, Pacheco Pass, the SJR refuge) and Mount Diablo.
- A script checked every string for word limits, sentence counts, direction words, dashes, hype words and exclamation marks: 0 problems. `tools/lint_content.py`: 0 errors, 0 warnings.
