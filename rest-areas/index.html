I-5 rest areas in California

Every Caltrans rest area on Interstate 5 in California, south to north, from the Mexico border to the Oregon line: 17 stops, with the milepost (the exit number) and which direction can use them. Truck stops and travel centers follow, for when a rest area is closed or far.

Caltrans closes rest areas for repairs from time to time; Caltrans QuickMap shows what is open right now. Going over a mountain pass? Is the Grapevine open?
