Newman on I-5 in California

Newman sits at about mile 423 of I-5 in California, near exit 423. The town of Newman, east of the freeway, was founded in 1888 by Simon Newman, a businessman who came to California from Bavaria as a teenager in 1862. Born Simon Neumann, he arrived speaking only German and Hebrew, learned English, and translated his family name. Below are the exits, services, landmarks and nearest rest areas around it.

Who founded Newman, California?

Simon Newman founded the town in 1888. Born Simon Neumann in Bavaria, he came to California in 1862 as a teenager speaking only German and Hebrew, then learned English and translated his family name.
