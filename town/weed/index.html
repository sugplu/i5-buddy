Weed on I-5 in California

Weed sits at about mile 747 of I-5 in California, near exit 747. Weed is named for Abner Weed, who found that the strong winds here were good for drying lumber. In 1897 he bought a mill and 280 acres for 400 dollars. Below are the exits, services, landmarks and nearest rest areas around it.

Weed, lumber and wind

Weed grew up around lumber mills. Hot flows from Shastina's eruptions once covered the ground here, and recent wildfires have left their marks.

Weed is at exit 747, which also serves US 97 toward Klamath Falls and South Weed Boulevard. Exit 745 is South Weed and exit 748 is North Weed Boulevard.
