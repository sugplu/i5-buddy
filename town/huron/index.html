Huron on I-5 in California

Huron is a farm town of about 6,200 people east of I-5 at exit 325, Jayne Avenue. It began in 1888 as a Southern Pacific water stop and swells to more than 15,000 at harvest time. The freeway here crosses the West Side farmland of Fresno County.

What to see near Huron

  1. Exit 325 · on your right going north · Towns

    Huron, harvest town

    East of the freeway is Huron, a farm town of about 6,200 people that swells to more than 15,000 at harvest time, when migrant farmworkers arrive. It began in 1888 as a water stop on the Southern Pacific Railroad. Full story

  2. Exit 322 · Orchards & farms

    Garlic country

    Fresno County grows most of the garlic in the United States, and west side towns such as Huron picked up the slack as Gilroy, once the country's biggest garlic producer, ran out of room. Full story

  3. Exit 325 · on your right going north · Oddities

    Gates Substation bottleneck

    The sprawling electrical yard east of the freeway is Gates Substation, a key junction on the power lines linking Northern and Southern California. Full story

  4. Exit 327 · History

    Arroyo Pasajero bridges

    The freeway bridges here cross Los Gatos Creek, once called Arroyo Pasajero, the travelers' creek. Full story

Fresno County's West Side

The freeway runs through the West Side farmland of Fresno County, which in 2022 led California counties in the value of its almonds, pistachios, garlic and processing tomatoes.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Huron on I-5?

Huron is at exit 325, Jayne Avenue, and the town lies east of the freeway.

Is there gas at the Huron exit on I-5?

Gas is mapped at exit 325 in OpenStreetMap data: three stations, with Shell and Valero among the brands listed. This is as mapped, not live, so it does not show whether any are open.

Where is the nearest rest area to Huron on I-5?

The Coalinga-Avenal Rest Area at mile 320.2 is about 4.8 miles south of Huron and serves both directions. Going north, the John "Chuck" Erreca Rest Area at mile 385.9 is about 60.9 miles ahead and also serves both directions.

How big is Huron, California?

Huron has about 6,200 people, rising to more than 15,000 at harvest time when migrant farmworkers arrive. It began in 1888 as a water stop on the Southern Pacific Railroad.

What is the big electrical yard near Huron on I-5?

It is Gates Substation, on the right going north around mile 324. It is a key junction on the power lines linking Northern and Southern California.
