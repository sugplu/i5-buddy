Woodland on I-5 in California

Woodland is the county seat of Yolo County and calls itself the City of Trees, for the valley oaks planted around town. Exit 537 is CA 113 South and Main Street, and exit 538 is CA 113 North toward Yuba City. Stops listed at both exits come from OpenStreetMap as mapped, not live.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Woodland on I-5?

Woodland is at exit 537, CA 113 South and Main Street. Exit 538, CA 113 North toward Yuba City, is the next one up.

Is there gas and EV charging in Woodland near I-5?

At exit 537, 5 gas stations are mapped, including 7-Eleven, Chevron and Quik Stop, and EV charging listed under 7-Eleven, EvGateway and Tesla Supercharger. At exit 538, one Chevron is mapped, with Blink and EvGateway charging listed. This is mapped data, not live.

Where can I eat or stay in Woodland off I-5?

Exit 537 has 13 food places mapped, such as Black Bear Diner, Chipotle and Denny's, and 4 lodging places, including Days Inn, Motel 6 and Quality Inn & Suites. Exit 538 has 2 food places, Corabella's Restaurant and Ranch Kitchen, and 2 lodging places, Best Western and Valley Oaks Inn.

What is the big plant near I-5 at Woodland?

It is the Woodland tomato cannery, about half a mile from the freeway at exit 537, on the left going north.

Where is the nearest rest area to Woodland on I-5?

Going north, Dunnigan Rest Area is 19.1 miles from here at mile 556.6, serving both directions. To the south, Elkhorn Rest Area is 8.2 miles away at mile 529.3.
