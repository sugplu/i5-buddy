Dunnigan on I-5 in California

Dunnigan sits at exit 556 on I-5 in farm country near the Dunnigan Hills. The town began as Antelope: after a flood in the winter of 1853 washed two settlers, Copp and Wilson, off their claims near the Sacramento River, they moved up to higher ground here. I-5 Buddy lists gas, EV charging, food and lodging at exits 554 and 556, as mapped in OpenStreetMap, not live.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Dunnigan on I-5?

Dunnigan is at exit 556 on I-5, which is mile 556.2 counting up from the Mexico border.

Is there a rest area near Dunnigan on I-5?

Yes. Dunnigan Rest Area is at mile 556.6, about 0.4 miles from the town, and serves both directions. Going the other way, the nearest rest area south of town in the data is Elkhorn Rest Area at mile 529.3, about 26.9 miles back.

Where can I get gas and EV charging near Dunnigan?

Exit 554 (County Road 8) lists five gas stations, including Pilot, Arco and Chevron, plus Tesla Supercharger and GM Energy charging. Exit 556 lists two gas stations, Chevron and Diamond, with ChargePoint and Electrify America charging. All of this is as mapped, not live.

Where can I eat or stay in Dunnigan?

Exit 554 lists six food places, including Burger King, Starbucks, Taco Bell and Original Mel's, and one lodging place, Hacienda Motel Dunnigan. Exit 556 lists Jack in the Box and Americas Best Value Inn. These are mapped listings, not live.

Why was Dunnigan once called Antelope?

Dunnigan began as Antelope. A flood in the winter of 1853 washed two settlers, Copp and Wilson, off their claims near the Sacramento River, and they moved up to higher ground here.
