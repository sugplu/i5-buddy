Oceanside on I-5 in California

Oceanside sits at about mile 54 of I-5 in California, near exit 54. Oceanside's wooden pier, 1,942 feet long, is the second-longest wooden pier on the West Coast. The first one went up in 1888, the year the city incorporated, and storms have wrecked it again and again. Below are the exits, services, landmarks and nearest rest areas around it.

  3. Exit 55 · History

    Camp Pendleton

    The open hills along this stretch belong to Marine Corps Base Camp Pendleton, more than 125,000 acres with 17 miles of coastline, the last major undeveloped stretch of California coast south of Santa Barbara.

  4. Exit 57 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Santa Margarita River

    The freeway crosses the Santa Margarita River, one of the last free-flowing rivers in Southern California.

Carlsbad and Oceanside

Carlsbad's flower and strawberry fields, three more lagoons, Batiquitos, Agua Hedionda and Buena Vista, and the beach town of Oceanside fill this stretch of Luiseño and Kumeyaay country.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Oceanside on I-5?

Oceanside and its pier are at exit 54. Nearby exits run from exit 52 (Oceanside Boulevard) and exit 53 (Mission Avenue) through exits 54A, 54B and 54C.

How long is the Oceanside Pier?

The wooden pier is 1,942 feet long, the second-longest wooden pier on the West Coast. The first pier went up in 1888, the year the city incorporated, and storms have wrecked it again and again.

What exit do I take for Mission San Luis Rey from I-5?

Exit 54 is Highway 76 east, the way to Mission San Luis Rey, about four and a half miles inland. It is on the right going north.

Where can I find gas and EV charging in Oceanside?

Gas is mapped at exits 52, 53 and 54C, and EV charging is mapped at exits 52, 53, 54A and 54C. These come from OpenStreetMap as listed, not live, so they may not show what is open or available now.

Where is the next rest area north of Oceanside on I-5?

The Aliso Creek Rest Area is at mile 59.4, about 5.4 miles north of here, and it serves both directions.
