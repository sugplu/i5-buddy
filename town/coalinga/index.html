Coalinga on I-5 in California

Coalinga is at exit 337 on Interstate 5, where CA 33 South and CA 145 North meet the freeway. The town began in 1888 as a Southern Pacific coaling stop, which the railroad called Coaling Station A. What to see near Coalinga

  1. Exit 337 · on your left going north · Towns

    Coalinga and the Iron Zoo

    Coalinga began in 1888 as a Southern Pacific coaling stop for steam locomotives. The railroad called it Coaling Station A. Local tradition says a railroad official made the name more musical by adding an a. It is just as likely that a small station sign squeezed Coaling A into Coalinga. Full story

  2. Exit 334 · on your right going north · Good stops

    Harris Ranch Inn and restaurant

    The Harris Ranch restaurant opened beside the freeway in 1977, aimed at local farmers, and soon became a popular stop for travelers on the busy freeway between Los Angeles and San Francisco. Full story

  3. Exit 334 · on your left going north · History

    Coalinga Oil Field

    The long, low ridge between the freeway and Coalinga is Anticline Ridge, part of the Coalinga Oil Field, one of only six California fields to have produced more than a billion barrels of oil. Full story

  4. Exit 337 · on your left going north · History

    The 1983 Coalinga earthquake

    At 4:42 on the afternoon of May 2, 1983, a magnitude 6.2 earthquake struck Coalinga. Full story

  5. Exit 337 · on your right going north · Orchards & farms

    Harris Ranch feedlot

    Harris Ranch is California's largest beef producer, and its feedlot sits right beside the freeway. Full story

  6. Exit 341 · Orchards & farms

    Westlands Water District

    Much of the west side of Fresno and Kings counties, along this part of the freeway, is served by Westlands Water District, formed in 1952. Full story

Coalinga plains

Wide-open farmland stretches east of the freeway, with the oil field ridges around Coalinga and the Diablo Range to the west, and the big Harris Ranch feedlot beside the freeway.

What exit is Coalinga on I-5?
