San Clemente on I-5 in California

San Clemente's hillside streets of Spanish-style buildings sit beside I-5 between exit 72 (Cristianitos Road) and exit 79 (CA 1). I-5 Buddy tells the story of Spanish Village by the Sea at exit 76, where Ole Hanson laid out the town in 1925. Gas, EV, food and lodging below are as mapped in OpenStreetMap, not live.

What to see near San Clemente

  1. Exit 76 · Towns

    Spanish Village by the Sea

    In 1925 Ole Hanson, a former mayor of Seattle, laid out a 2,000-acre town on old rancho land by the ocean and named it after San Clemente Island. He added a clause to the deeds sending every building plan to an architectural review board, to keep roofs of red tile and walls of white.

  2. Exit 72 · on your left going north · History

    Nixon's Western White House

    On the bluff at Cotton's Point stands La Casa Pacifica, the house of peace, which President Richard Nixon bought in 1969.

  3. Exit 72 · on your right going north · History

    Panhe and Los Cristianitos

    Inland of the freeway, in the San Mateo campground area of San Onofre State Beach, is Panhe, the place at the water.

  4. Exit 79 · on your left going north · History

    Dana Point and the hides

    West of the freeway on Pacific Coast Highway, Dana Point is named for Richard Henry Dana Jr., who sailed here in 1835 on the brig Pilgrim and wrote about it in Two Years Before the Mast.

San Onofre and San Clemente

The freeway runs above San Onofre's beaches and the surf breaks of Trestles at the north edge of Camp Pendleton. The Orange County line is at Cristianitos Road, beside the hillside streets of Spanish-style San Clemente.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is San Clemente on I-5?

The exit marked San Clemente is exit 75, at Avenida Presidio and Avenida Palizada. The town is also reached from exits 72 through 79, including exit 74 (El Camino Real) and exit 76 (Avenida Pico).

Where is gas and EV charging in San Clemente off I-5?

Gas is mapped at exits 73 through 79, with the most stations (3 each) at exits 76 and 79. EV charging is mapped at exits 75 through 79, including a Tesla Supercharger and ChargePoint at exit 77, EVgo at exit 78 and Electrify America at exit 79. This is as mapped, not live.

Where can I eat in San Clemente near I-5?

Food is mapped at exits 72 through 78. Exit 75 has the most places listed (65), followed by exit 76 (29) and exit 78 (22). Examples include Antoine's Cafe at exit 75 and Ruby's Diner at exit 77.

Where can I stay in San Clemente near I-5?

Lodging is mapped at exits 72 through 76 and at exit 79. Exit 73 lists 7 places, including Hampton Inn & Suites, and exit 79 lists 7, including Best Western and Hilton Garden Inn.

Is there a rest area near San Clemente on I-5?

The nearest rest area south of town is Aliso Creek Rest Area at mile 59.4, 16.4 miles away, open to both directions. North of town the nearest is Tejon Pass Rest Area at mile 206.1, 130.3 miles away.
