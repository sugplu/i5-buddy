What to see near Santa Ana

  1. Exit 105 · on your left going north · Towns

    Santa Ana, the county seat

    West of the freeway, Santa Ana grew on Rancho Santiago de Santa Ana, granted in 1810 to José Antonio Yorba, a sergeant in the Spanish army. His rancho took in the land of today's Santa Ana, Orange, Tustin, Irvine and Costa Mesa.

  2. Exit 103 · Orchards & farms

    Orange County's orange groves

    In 1936 Orange County grew a sixth of the nation's Valencia oranges, and by 1948 it had 67,263 acres of them, more than five million trees.

  3. Exit 104 · Good stops

    Santa Ana Zoo's 50 monkeys

    Right beside the freeway, in Prentice Park, is the Santa Ana Zoo, which opened on March 8, 1952.

  4. Exit 107 · Land & water

    Santa Ana River

    The wide channel under the freeway here is the Santa Ana River, the largest river entirely within Southern California, running 96 miles from the San Bernardino Mountains to the sea.

Santa Ana and Anaheim

The freeway crosses the Santa Ana River and the Orange Crush interchange and passes Christ Cathedral and Disneyland, on a plain that was vineyards in the 1800s and orange groves into the 1950s.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Santa Ana on I-5?

Santa Ana, the county seat, is at exit 105 on I-5. Exit 105A is 17th Street and exit 105B is Main Street and Broadway.

Where can I get gas near Santa Ana on I-5?

Gas is mapped at several exits nearby. Exit 101B lists Arco, Chevron, Mobil, Shell and Valero, exit 103A lists Arco, SC Fuels, Shell and Speeed Gas, and exit 107B lists 7-Eleven and Ralphs.

Where are EV chargers near Santa Ana on I-5?

Mapped chargers include a Tesla Supercharger at exits 101B, 104B, 105A and 106, EVgo at exit 104, and ChargePoint and Rove at exit 105A. Exit 107B also lists an EV charger.

Is there a zoo near I-5 in Santa Ana?

Yes. The Santa Ana Zoo is right beside the freeway in Prentice Park, near exit 104, and it opened on March 8, 1952.

Where is the nearest rest area to Santa Ana on I-5?

The nearest listed rest area south is Aliso Creek Rest Area at mile 59.4, 45.6 miles away. North is Tejon Pass Rest Area at mile 206.1, 101.1 miles away, and both serve both directions.
