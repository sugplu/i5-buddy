La Mirada on I-5 in California

La Mirada sits at about mile 118 of I-5 in California, near exit 118. La Mirada, Spanish for the look, began as the ranch of Andrew McNally, the Chicago printer and mapmaker of Rand McNally. In 1888 he bought more than 2,200 acres for $200,000 and surrounded his home, Windermere Ranch, with olive, orange and lemon groves. What to see near La Mirada

  1. Exit 118 · on your right going north · Towns

    La Mirada's olive ranch

    La Mirada, Spanish for the look, began as the ranch of Andrew McNally, the Chicago printer and mapmaker of Rand McNally. In 1888 he bought more than 2,200 acres for $200,000 and surrounded his home, Windermere Ranch, with olive, orange and lemon groves.

  2. Exit 116 · on your left going north · Good stops

    Knott's chicken and boysenberries

    About a mile and a half south on Beach Boulevard, Knott's Berry Farm began in 1923 as a roadside berry stand.

  3. Exit 121 · on your left going north · History

    Norwalk and Rancho Los Nietos

    In 1784 the Spanish governor gave a former soldier, Manuel Nieto, the use of all the land between the Santa Ana and Los Angeles rivers, from Mission San Gabriel down to the sea.

Buena Park to Norwalk

Between Buena Park and Downey the freeway crosses the line between Orange and Los Angeles counties and runs through the Gateway Cities of La Mirada, Santa Fe Springs and Norwalk, on Rancho Los Nietos land that later held olive groves, dairies and oil fields.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is La Mirada on I-5?

La Mirada is at exit 118, Valley View Avenue and N Firestone Blvd, at mile 117.8. The exits just before it going north are exit 117 (Artesia Boulevard) and exit 116 (CA 39, Beach Boulevard); the next is exit 119 (Carmenita Road).

Where is gas near La Mirada on I-5?

Gas is mapped at exit 118, where an Arco is listed. Others are listed at exit 117 (Mobil, SC Fuels), exit 120B (76), exit 120 (Soniz) and exit 121 (Chevron, Shell). This comes from OpenStreetMap and is not live, so check hours and prices yourself.

Is there EV charging near La Mirada on I-5?

EVgo is mapped at exit 118. Nearby, ChargePoint and Electrify America are mapped at exit 116, a Tesla Supercharger at exit 115, and ChargePoint and EVgo at exit 121. These are listings, not live availability.

Where can I stay or eat near La Mirada on I-5?

Exit 118 has the most lodging listed nearby, 18 places including Extended Stay America, Holiday Inn and Marriott Residence Inn. Food mapped there includes Chick-fil-A, In-N-Out Burger, North Woods Inn, Starbucks and Subway.
