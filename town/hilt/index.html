Hilt on I-5 in California

Hilt sits at exit 796 on I-5 and has the northernmost exit in California. It began as a company town, first for the Northern California Lumber Company and then for the Fruit Growers Supply Company, an affiliate of Sunkist. The 42nd parallel, which marks the California and Oregon border, is at mile 797.

Klamath River to the Oregon line

Below the old Iron Gate Dam site, the Klamath runs through an arid valley of farms and ranches. To the north rise the Siskiyou Mountains, the divide between the Klamath and the Rogue.

Where is the California and Oregon border on I-5?

The border follows the 42nd parallel, which crosses I-5 at mile 797, just past the Hilt exit. The line was set in an 1819 treaty between the United States and Spain.
