Gerber on I-5 in California

Gerber is a Tehama County stop on I-5 reached at exit 642 (Proberta, Gerber, Flores Avenue). East of the freeway, the town was platted in 1910 and served as a Southern Pacific Railroad division point from 1916 to 1965. This is the northern end of a band of farmland that runs almost unbroken to Bakersfield.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Gerber on I-5?

Gerber is at exit 642 on I-5, signed as Proberta, Gerber, Flores Avenue. The town itself lies east of the freeway.

What is the history of Gerber, California?

Gerber was platted in 1910 and named for the Gerber family, who owned the town site. From 1916 to 1965 it was a Southern Pacific Railroad division point where steam locomotives took on fuel, got repairs and changed crews.

Where is the nearest rest area to Gerber on I-5?

The Lt. John C. Helmick Rest Area is 7.6 miles south, and the Herbert S. Miles Rest Area is 14.2 miles north. Both serve traffic in both directions.

Which towns are before and after Gerber on I-5?
