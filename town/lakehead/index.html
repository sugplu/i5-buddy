Lakehead on I-5 in California

Lakehead sits at about mile 701 of I-5 in California, near exit 702. Lakehead sits on the northern shore of Shasta Lake. About 470 people lived here in 2020, and in summer more than 1,500 seasonal residents can join them. Below are the exits, services, landmarks and nearest rest areas around it.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Lakehead on I-5?

Lakehead is at exit 702, Lakeshore Drive. The nearby exits are 698 (Gillman Road; Salt Creek Road) and 704 (Riverview Drive).

Where are the rest areas near Lakehead on I-5?

The Lakehead Rest Area is at mile 704.7, 3.5 miles north of town. To the south, O'Brien Rest Area is at mile 692.9, 8.3 miles from here. The data does not state which direction each one serves.

What happened near Lakehead's Salt Creek exit in 2021?

On June 30, 2021, a fire started beside I-5 near the Salt Creek exit (exit 698) and shut down the freeway. It is called the Salt Fire, and the landmark sits on the right going north.
