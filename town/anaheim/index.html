Anaheim on I-5 in California

Anaheim sits on I-5 around exit 111, where the freeway crosses what was once farmland: German families founded the town in 1857 to grow grapes. Exits 109 through 110B serve Katella Avenue, Harbor Boulevard and Disneyland Drive, with Disneyland just southwest of the freeway near exit 110. What to see near Anaheim

  1. Exit 111 · on your right going north · Towns

    Anaheim's German vineyards

    Anaheim was founded in 1857 by 50 German families from San Francisco, who bought some 1,160 acres near the Santa Ana River for 2 dollars an acre to grow grapes. They named it Annaheim, home by the Santa Ana River.

  2. Exit 107 · on your left going north · Oddities

    The Crystal Cathedral

    The huge glass building west of the freeway is Christ Cathedral, built as the Crystal Cathedral and finished in 1980.

  3. Exit 109 · on your right going north · Good stops

    Angel Stadium and the Big A

    East of the freeway, near Katella Avenue, is Angel Stadium, home of the Los Angeles Angels since 1966.

  4. Exit 110 · on your left going north · Good stops

    Disneyland

    Just southwest of the freeway is Disneyland, built on 160 acres of orange groves and walnut trees, a site picked by researchers Disney hired to forecast where the region's population would grow.

Santa Ana and Anaheim

The freeway crosses the Santa Ana River and the Orange Crush interchange and passes Christ Cathedral and Disneyland, on a plain that was vineyards in the 1800s and orange groves into the 1950s.

Nearest rest areas

  • South of town: Aliso Creek Rest Area, mile 59, 51 miles from Anaheim (both directions).
  • North of town: Tejon Pass Rest Area, mile 206, 95 miles from Anaheim (both directions).

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Disneyland on I-5?

Disneyland is just southwest of the freeway near exit 110, on the left going north. Exit 110B is signed Disneyland Drive, Ball Road.

What exit is Angel Stadium on I-5?

Angel Stadium is east of the freeway near Katella Avenue at exit 109, on the right going north.
