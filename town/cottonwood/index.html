Cottonwood on I-5 in California

Cottonwood is a stagecoach town settled in 1849, sitting halfway between Redding and Red Bluff, 15 miles from each, on both banks of Cottonwood Creek. I-5 Buddy's talking guide marks it at exit 664, with exits 662 and 665 close by. Gas, EV and food stops below are listed in OpenStreetMap data, not live.

What to see near Cottonwood

  1. Exit 664 · on your right going north · Towns

    Cottonwood, the halfway town

    Cottonwood was a stagecoach town, settled in 1849, and it got its first post office in 1852. It sits halfway between Redding and Red Bluff, 15 miles from each, on both banks of Cottonwood Creek, which gave it its name. Full story

  2. Exit 662 · Oddities

    Cottonwood truck scales

    The truck scales here are the Cottonwood Commercial Vehicle Enforcement Facility, run by the California Highway Patrol. Full story

  3. Exit 662 · Land & water

    Cottonwood Creek

    The freeway crosses Cottonwood Creek here, which forms the line between Tehama and Shasta counties along its whole length. Full story

  4. Exit 665 · on your right going north · Land & water

    Coleman fish hatchery

    About 7 miles east, on Battle Creek, is Coleman National Fish Hatchery, the largest salmon hatchery in the continental United States. Full story

Cottonwood and Anderson

Near the northern tip of the Central Valley, the freeway passes Cottonwood and Anderson, with ranch land along Cottonwood Creek.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Cottonwood on I-5?

The Cottonwood landmark is at exit 664, which is Gas Point Road and Balls Ferry Road. Exit 665 is also marked Cottonwood, and exit 662 is Bowman Road, Cottonwood.

Is there gas on I-5 at Cottonwood?

Gas is mapped at two exits: a Chevron at exit 662 and a Chevron at exit 664. This comes from OpenStreetMap, so it is not a live check of what is open.

Is there an EV charger near Cottonwood on I-5?

A Tesla Supercharger is mapped at exit 664, Gas Point Road and Balls Ferry Road. The data does not say how many chargers there are or whether they are available now.

Where can I eat in Cottonwood off I-5?

Exit 664 lists three places in the map data: Cottonwood Eatery, Jake's Take & Bake Pizza and Subway. Exit 665 has one more food place mapped, listed only as Food.

Where is the nearest rest area to Cottonwood on I-5?

South of town, the Herbert S. Miles Rest Area is at mile 654.1, 9.8 miles from here, and serves both directions. North of town, the O'Brien Rest Area is at mile 692.9, 29.0 miles away.
