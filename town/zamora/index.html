Zamora on I-5 in California

Zamora is a farm community at exit 548 on Interstate 5, once called Black's after J. J. Black, who settled here in 1865. When the Northern Railway built north from Woodland in the 1870s, Black gave land for a depot, and a small town grew around it. I-5 Buddy's talking guide tells this story as you drive past.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Zamora on I-5?

Zamora is at exit 548 on I-5 in California. Exit numbers count up from the Mexico border.

Why was Zamora once called Black's?

The town was named for J. J. Black, who settled here in 1865. He later donated a strip of land for a railway depot when the Northern Railway built its line north from Woodland in the 1870s.

Where is the nearest rest area to Zamora on I-5?

Going north, Dunnigan Rest Area is at mile 556.6, 8.4 miles from Zamora, and serves both directions. To the south, Elkhorn Rest Area is at mile 529.3, 18.9 miles away; the data does not say which direction it serves.

What is the countryside like around Zamora on I-5?

It is farm country near the Dunnigan Hills. Some fields nearby grow sunflowers for planting seed, and on clear days the Sutter Buttes rise from the valley floor to the north.
