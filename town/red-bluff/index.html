Red Bluff on I-5 in California

Red Bluff sits at about mile 649 of I-5 in California, near exit 649. Red Bluff is named for red bluffs that once stood out along the banks of the Sacramento River, which the freeway crosses twice in town, less than two miles apart. The town grew up at the head of navigation, the farthest point riverboats could reach, and miners landed here bound for the gold fields of Trinity County.
