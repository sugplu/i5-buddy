National City on I-5 in California

National City takes its name from El Rancho del Rey, the Ranch of the King, where Spanish soldiers grazed their horses on 26,000 acres near exit 11. I-5 here follows the east side of San Diego Bay, past the Navy's piers, Barrio Logan and downtown. Gas, EV, food and lodging below come from OpenStreetMap as mapped, not live.

What to see near National City

  1. Exit 11 · Towns

    National City's royal ranch

    National City takes its name from a ranch. Spanish soldiers grazed their horses on 26,000 acres here called El Rancho del Rey, the Ranch of the King.

  2. Exit 7 · Orchards & farms

    Chula Vista, the old Lemon Capital

    Chula Vista is Spanish for beautiful view, a name suggested by the designer of the Sweetwater Dam.

  3. Exit 8 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Sweetwater Marsh

    West of the freeway, where the Sweetwater River meets San Diego Bay, 316 acres of salt marsh and coastal upland form part of a national wildlife refuge.

  4. Exit 13 · on your left going north · History

    Naval Base San Diego

    The Navy ships along the bay belong to Naval Base San Diego, the main home port of the US Pacific Fleet.

  5. Exit 14 · on your left going north · History

    Coronado Bridge and Chicano Park

    The long, curving bridge over the bay is the San Diego-Coronado Bridge, opened in 1969.

San Diego Bay and downtown

This stretch follows the east side of San Diego Bay through National City, the Navy's piers, Barrio Logan, downtown, the airport and Old Town.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is National City on I-5?

The National City landmark sits at exit 11, and nearby exits run from 7A at L Street through 15A at CA 94 East. Exit 11B is Plaza Boulevard, Downtown and 8th Street, National City, and exit 12 is Division Street and Main Street.

Where is gas near National City on I-5?

Gas is mapped at exit 7A (Costco Gasoline), exit 8A (76), exit 9 (ARCO), exit 11B (Chevron) and exit 13B (NEX Autoport). This is listed data from OpenStreetMap, not live.

Where are EV chargers near National City on I-5?

EV chargers are mapped at several exits: Tesla Supercharger at exits 9 and 13B, Electrify America at 11B, ChargePoint at 12, EVgo at 13A and Blink at 15A. Exit 10 also lists an EV charger. This is listed data, not live.

Where can I find food and lodging near National City on I-5?

Food is mapped at most exits, with 31 places at exit 8A (H Street) and 12 at exit 13B. Lodging is mapped at exits 7B, 8A, 8B, 9, 10, 12, 13A, 13B and 15A, with 9 places at exit 12 (Main Street, National City Boulevard).

Where is the Navy base near National City on I-5?

Naval Base San Diego is at about exit 13, on the left going north. It is the main home port of the US Pacific Fleet.
