Mendota on I-5 in California

Mendota sits at about mile 349 of I-5 in California, near exit 349. Highway 33 leads from here to Mendota, known as the Cantaloupe Center of the World. It began to thrive in 1891 as a Southern Pacific storage and switching yard, and the railroad borrowed its name from Mendota, Illinois. Questions drivers ask

What exit is Mendota on I-5?

Mendota is at exit 349, signed CA 33 North; Derrick Avenue; Mendota. Highway 33 leads from the exit to the town.

What is there to see near Mendota on I-5?

Going north, three stone pillars called the Joaquin Rocks stand on the ridge to the left near exit 345, about 4,100 feet up. Near exit 349, Arroyo de Cantua lies about a mile and a half west of the freeway on the left, said to be a hideout of the Gold Rush bandit Joaquin Murrieta.

Where is the nearest rest area to Mendota on I-5?

The Coalinga-Avenal Rest Area is at mile 320.2, about 28.8 miles south of Mendota, and serves both directions. The John "Chuck" Erreca Rest Area is at mile 385.9, about 36.9 miles north, also serving both directions.

What kind of farmland surrounds Mendota?

Big farms of the Westlands Water District spread across the valley floor here, growing crops such as almonds, tomatoes, pistachios and wheat.
