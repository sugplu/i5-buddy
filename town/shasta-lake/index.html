    Shasta Lake, the dam towns

    The city of Shasta Lake grew out of five small communities that sprang up in 1938, when work began on Shasta Dam: Central Valley, Project City, Summit City, Pine Grove and Toyon. Pine Grove stood where Pine Grove Avenue now meets the freeway. Full story

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Shasta Lake on I-5?

The main Shasta Lake landmark in I-5 Buddy is at exit 684, Pine Grove Avenue. Nearby exits are 682 (Oasis Road), 685 (CA 151, Shasta Dam Boulevard) and 687 (Mountain Gate).

What exit do I take for Shasta Dam from I-5?

Take exit 685, CA 151 (Shasta Dam Boulevard). The dam stands about four miles west of the freeway and is 602 feet tall, and it is on the left going north.

Where is gas near Shasta Lake on I-5?

Gas is listed at exit 682 (Arco, Shell and one other), exit 684 (Valero), exit 685 (76, Arco and Chevron) and exit 687 (Chevron and Shell). This is as mapped, not live.

Is there food and lodging at the Shasta Dam Boulevard exit?

Exit 685 lists Burger King, Latino's Mexican Food, McDonald's, Oriental Express and Taco Shop, plus the Shasta Dam Motel. Listings are from OpenStreetMap and are not live.

Where is the nearest rest area to Shasta Lake on I-5?

The O'Brien Rest Area at mile 692.9 is about 8 miles north of here. South of town, the Herbert S. Miles Rest Area at mile 654.1 serves both directions, about 30.8 miles back.
