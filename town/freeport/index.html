Freeport on I-5 in California

Freeport is a tiny river town just west of I-5, with its landmark at exit 510. It began in 1862 as a way around Sacramento's port taxes, and the freeway here runs past the Cosumnes River Preserve wetlands and along the edge of the Stone Lakes refuge.

What to see near Freeport

    Freeport, a town born of taxes

    The tiny river town of Freeport, just west of the freeway, began as a way around Sacramento's port taxes. In 1862, businessmen tired of paying taxes at Sacramento's riverfront landing formed the Freeport Railroad Company to build a new port, free of those taxes, with a railroad that bypassed Sacramento.

Cosumnes wetlands and Elk Grove

Wetlands of the Cosumnes River Preserve lie near the freeway, which runs along the eastern edge of the Stone Lakes refuge as it passes through Elk Grove.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Freeport on I-5?

Freeport is named at exit 512, Pocket Road, Meadowview Road, Freeport. The town's landmark, Freeport, a town born of taxes, is placed at exit 510, Cosumnes River Boulevard, with the town just west of the freeway.

How did Freeport get its name?

In 1862, businessmen tired of paying taxes at Sacramento's riverfront landing formed the Freeport Railroad Company to build a new port free of those taxes. The railroad bypassed Sacramento, and the river town of Freeport grew from it.

Is there gas and EV charging near Freeport on I-5?

In the mapped data, exit 510 lists Arco and Chevron gas plus Shell, Tesla Supercharger and bp pulse charging networks. Exit 512 lists Chevron and Shell gas plus ChargePoint and another EV charger. This is listed data, not live, so it does not show what is open.

Where can I eat near Freeport on I-5?

Exit 510 has the most food mapped, 26 places including Chick-fil-A, Chipotle and Casa Flores. Exit 512 lists 13 places such as IHOP and Starbucks, and exit 513 lists 20 including Burger King and Carl's Jr.

Where is the nearest rest area to Freeport on I-5?

Going north, the Elkhorn Rest Area is at mile 529.3, 19.5 miles from Freeport. Going south, the Westley Rest Area is at mile 444.9, 64.9 miles away and serves both directions.
