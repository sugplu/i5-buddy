Burbank on I-5 in California

I-5 passes through Burbank, commonly known as the Media Capital of the World, with Warner Bros. and the Walt Disney Company headquartered in the Media District a couple of miles west of the freeway. The town sits on the flat east end of the San Fernando Valley, below the rocky Verdugo Mountains. I-5 Buddy lists the exits, mapped services and landmarks here so you can plan a stop; it is not live traffic or business information.

What to see near Burbank

  1. Exit 147 · Towns

    Burbank, the Media Capital

    Burbank, which the freeway passes through, is commonly known as the Media Capital of the World. Warner Bros. and the Walt Disney Company both have their headquarters here, in the Media District, a couple of miles west of the freeway. Full story

  2. Exit 144 · on your left going north · History

    The 1933 Griffith Park fire

    On October 3, 1933, a brush fire in Griffith Park killed at least 29 men who volunteered or were ordered to fight it. Full story

  3. Exit 144 · Good stops

    Zoo, Autry and Travel Town

    Three family stops sit along the north side of Griffith Park: the Los Angeles Zoo, the Autry Museum of the American West, and Travel Town, a railroad museum with 43 full-size engines, cars and other rolling stock. Full story

  4. Exit 145 · on your right going north · History

    Grand Central Air Terminal

    Just east of the freeway stands the 1928 terminal building of Grand Central Airport, once a major gateway to Los Angeles by air. Full story

  5. Exit 146 · on your right going north · History

    Verdugo Mountains

    The rocky range rising east of the freeway is the Verdugo Mountains, topping out at 3,126 feet. Full story

  6. Exit 149 · on your left going north · History

    Lockheed's hidden factory

    During World War II, Lockheed's aircraft factory beside the Burbank airport was disguised from the air. Full story

Burbank and Sun Valley

Studios, the airport and the neighborhoods of Glendale, Burbank and Sun Valley spread across the flat east end of the San Fernando Valley, below the rocky Verdugo Mountains.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Burbank on I-5?

The Burbank, the Media Capital landmark is at exit 147. Nearby exits include 146B (Burbank Boulevard), 147B (Empire Avenue, San Fernando Boulevard) and 148 (Buena Vista Street).

Where can I find gas and EV charging in Burbank on I-5?

In the mapped data, gas is listed at exits 144, 145A, 145B, 146A, 146B, 147B and 148. EV charging is mapped at exit 146A (ChargePoint), 146B (Tesla Supercharger), 147B (Electrify America and Tesla Supercharger) and 148 (ChargePoint); none of this is live.

Where can I eat or find a hotel near I-5 in Burbank?

Food is mapped at most Burbank exits, with the most places listed at exit 146B (80) and exit 146A (42). Lodging is mapped at exits 145A, 145B, 146A, 147B and 148, including Hotel Burbank at exit 146A and Courtyard at exit 147B.

What is there to see near I-5 in Burbank?

Near exit 144 are the Los Angeles Zoo, the Autry Museum of the American West and Travel Town, a railroad museum with 43 full-size engines, cars and other rolling stock. Going north, the Verdugo Mountains rise on the right near mile 146.4, and the 1928 Grand Central Air Terminal building stands just east of the freeway.
