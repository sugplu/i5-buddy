Pacoima on I-5 in California

Pacoima is at exit 155 on I-5, in the north San Fernando Valley with Mission Hills and Sylmar. Its name comes from Pakoinga, a Native village name meaning entrance, and it is the hometown of Ritchie Valens, born Richard Valenzuela here in 1941. I-5 Buddy's talking guide covers the exits and stops around town, as mapped in OpenStreetMap data and not live.

What to see near Pacoima

  1. Exit 155 · on your right going north · Towns

    Pacoima, Ritchie Valens' hometown

    Pacoima takes its name from Pakoinga, the name of a Native village here meaning entrance; Spanish ears heard its ng sound as an m. It is also the hometown of Ritchie Valens, born Richard Valenzuela here in 1941, who turned the Mexican folk song La Bamba into a rock and roll hit in 1958. Full story

  2. Exit 157 · History

    Mission San Fernando

    Exit 157 is San Fernando Mission Boulevard, and Mission San Fernando is about a mile west of it. Full story

  3. Exit 158 · on your left going north · History

    The dam that almost failed

    The reservoirs just west of the freeway once supplied about 80 percent of Los Angeles' water. Full story

  4. Exit 159 · Orchards & farms

    Sylmar's sea of olives

    Sylmar's name, adopted in 1893, joins Latin words for forest and sea, for the sea of olive trees that covered these slopes. Full story

North San Fernando Valley

Pacoima, Mission Hills and Sylmar fill the north end of the valley, once a land of mission vineyards, wheat and olive groves, with the San Gabriel Mountains rising close by.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Pacoima on I-5?

The Pacoima landmark is at exit 155, with exit 155A at Terra Bella Street and exit 155B at Van Nuys Boulevard.

Why is Pacoima called Pacoima?

The name comes from Pakoinga, a Native village here whose name means entrance. Spanish ears heard its ng sound as an m.

Who is famous from Pacoima?

Ritchie Valens, born Richard Valenzuela in Pacoima in 1941. In 1958 he turned the Mexican folk song La Bamba into a rock and roll hit.

Where can I get gas and food near Pacoima on I-5?

Gas and food are listed at several nearby exits, including 154 (Osborne Street), 155A (Terra Bella Street) and 155B (Van Nuys Boulevard), where 7 food places are mapped. Exit 157 in San Fernando lists the most gas stations, 8, as mapped and not live.

Is there an EV charger near Pacoima on I-5?

EVgo is listed at exit 154 (Osborne Street). A Tesla Supercharger is listed at exit 157, and exit 157B lists Tesla and other EV chargers, as mapped and not live.
