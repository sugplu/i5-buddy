Stockton on I-5 in California

I-5 runs through Stockton past the deepwater port and its channel to San Francisco Bay, and crosses the Calaveras River. The Stockton exits run from 469 to 476, with gas, food, EV charging and lodging listed at several of them. Charles Weber laid out a town here in 1849 and called it Tuleburg before renaming it for Commodore Robert Stockton.

What to see near Stockton

  1. Exit 472 · on your right going north · Towns

    Stockton, once Tuleburg

    When Charles Weber laid out a town here in 1849, he called it Tuleburg. He soon renamed it for Commodore Robert Stockton, a famed naval commander of the Mexican-American War.

  2. Exit 470 · on your right going north · History

    First Sikh temple in America

    About a mile east of the freeway, on South Grant Street, is the first Sikh house of worship in the United States.

  3. Exit 472 · History

    Little Manila

    Highway 4 heads east from here on the Crosstown Freeway, built in the early 1970s through part of Little Manila.

  4. Exit 473 · on your left going north · History

    Port of Stockton

    The docks just west of the freeway belong to the Port of Stockton, a seaport more than 70 nautical miles from the ocean.

  5. Exit 474 · on your right going north · History

    University of the Pacific

    About a mile and a half east of the freeway is the University of the Pacific.

  6. Exit 476 · Land & water

    Calaveras River, river of skulls

    The freeway crosses the Calaveras River here.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is downtown Stockton on I-5?

Exit 472 is CA 4 East, Fresno Avenue and Navy Drive, signed for Downtown Stockton. It is the exit the data uses for Stockton itself.

Where can I get gas in Stockton on I-5?

Gas is listed at exits 469, 470, 471, 473, 474B and 476. Exit 471 (CA 4 West, Charter Way) has the most, with 7 gas stations mapped, including Chevron, Valero, Arco, Phillips 66 and Shell. This comes from OpenStreetMap as mapped, not live.

Is there EV charging in Stockton on I-5?

The data lists a Tesla Supercharger at exit 476 (March Lane) and an EVCS charger at exit 472 (Fresno Avenue, Navy Drive). These are as mapped in OpenStreetMap, not live.

Where are the hotels and motels in Stockton on I-5?

Lodging is listed at exits 470, 471, 472, 473 and 476. Exit 476 (March Lane) has the most, with 7 places mapped, including Courtyard, Extended Stay America and Hilton, while exit 471 has 4, including Days Inn Stockton and Motel 6.

What is the Port of Stockton on I-5?

At exit 473 the docks just west of the freeway, on the left going north, belong to the Port of Stockton. It is a seaport more than 70 nautical miles from the ocean.
