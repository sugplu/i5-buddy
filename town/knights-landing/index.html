Knights Landing on I-5 in California

County Road 102 at exit 536 leads north to Knights Landing, on the Sacramento River. Dr. William Knight, a physician from Baltimore, founded the town in 1843 on a mound that had long been a meeting place for Native people living around Cache Creek and the river. I-5 Buddy tells this story aloud as you drive, and lists gas, EV, food and lodging as mapped in OpenStreetMap, not live.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Knights Landing on I-5?

Knights Landing is at exit 536, signed as Road 102, County Road 102. The road leads north from the exit to the town on the Sacramento River.

What gas, food and lodging is mapped at exit 536?

The data lists 4 gas stations, including 76, Arco, Chevron and Costco, and 12 food places, such as Dutch Bros. Coffee, In-N-Out Burger and Jack in the Box. It also lists 4 lodging places, including Comfort Suites, Fairfield Inn & Suites, Hampton Inn & Suites and Holiday Inn Express. This is as mapped, not live.

Are there EV chargers at the Knights Landing exit?

Exit 536 has mapped EV charger listings from ChargePoint, EvGateway and Tesla Supercharger, plus a general EV charger listing. The data does not say how many chargers there are or whether they are working.

Where is the nearest rest area to Knights Landing on I-5?

Heading south, the Elkhorn Rest Area is at mile 529.3, 6.7 miles from here. Heading north, the Dunnigan Rest Area is at mile 556.6, 20.6 miles away, and it serves both directions.

What is the Yolo Bypass near Knights Landing?

The Yolo Bypass is a floodway 41 miles long and about 3 miles wide, crossed by the Elkhorn Causeway near exit 531. Between floods, much of it is farmed, mostly in rice.
