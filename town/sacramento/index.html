What to see near Sacramento

  1. Exit 518 · Towns

    Sacramento, the state capital

    Sacramento has been California's capital since 1854. The new state's lawmakers first met in San Jose, moved to Vallejo in 1852 and to Benicia in 1853, and then settled here.

  2. Exit 516 · on your right going north · Good stops

    Sacramento Zoo and Land Park

    The Sutterville Road exit leads to William Land Park and the Sacramento Zoo, a few blocks east of the freeway.

  3. Exit 519 · on your left going north · History

    Tower Bridge

    The gold towers just west of the freeway belong to the Tower Bridge, a lift bridge over the Sacramento River dedicated in 1935. Full story

  4. Exit 519 · Oddities

    The Boat Section

    Through downtown Sacramento, I-5 runs below the water table, close beside the Sacramento River. Full story

  5. Exit 519 · Good stops

    California State Railroad Museum

    The California State Railroad Museum, in Old Sacramento, holds 21 restored locomotives and railroad cars, some dating back to 1862. Full story

  6. Exit 521 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Where two rivers meet

    The freeway crosses the American River here, close to where it flows into the Sacramento. Full story

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Sacramento on I-5?

The Sacramento landmark is at exit 518, where I-5 meets US 50 and I 80 East. Downtown exits follow at 519A (Q Street) and 519B (J Street).

Which I-5 exit is closest to downtown Sacramento?

Exit 519B, J Street, is the downtown exit. The data lists 57 food places and 20 lodging places mapped there, plus ChargePoint and Tesla Supercharger listings.

Where is the Tower Bridge on I-5?

The gold towers of the Tower Bridge are just west of the freeway near exit 519, on the left going north. It is a lift bridge over the Sacramento River, dedicated in 1935.

Which exit goes to the Sacramento Zoo?

Exit 516, Sutterville Road, leads to William Land Park and the Sacramento Zoo, a few blocks east of the freeway. The zoo side is on the right going north.
