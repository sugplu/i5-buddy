Mount Shasta on I-5 in California

Mount Shasta City sits at exit 738 on I-5, where the canyon opens onto the foot of the volcano, with Black Butte close by. The town was once called Strawberry Valley, then Berryvale, then Sisson, and became the City of Mount Shasta in 1925. I-5 Buddy talks you through the area as you drive north.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Mount Shasta on I-5?

Mount Shasta City is at exit 738, Central Mount Shasta. Exit 740 is signed Mount Shasta and exit 741 is Abrams Lake Road.

Is there gas, EV charging, food and lodging at the Mount Shasta exit?

At exit 738, the data lists three gas stations (76, Chevron and Valero), EV charging from ChargePoint, Tesla Supercharger, Rivian Adventure Network and Rivian Waypoints, 25 food places and 10 lodging places. This comes from OpenStreetMap as mapped, not live, so hours and availability are not shown.

What is there to see near Mount Shasta on I-5?

Going north, the Mount Shasta trout hatchery is on the left at exit 737 and has raised trout since 1888. The headwaters of the Sacramento at Big Springs in Mount Shasta City Park are on the right at exit 739, and Mount Shasta itself, 14,179 feet, is on the right at exit 740.

Where is the nearest rest area to Mount Shasta on I-5?

Weed Airport Rest Area is 15.0 miles north of town at mile 753.1, serving both directions. Lakehead Rest Area is 33.4 miles south at mile 704.7.
