Los Banos on I-5 in California

Los Banos is a city about 7 miles east of Interstate 5, reached from exit 399. Its name is Spanish for the baths, after pools known as the baths of Padre Arroyo. This page shows the landmarks near the exit and the nearest rest areas listed on I-5 Buddy.

    Los Banos, the baths

    Los Banos, the city about 7 miles east of the freeway, is Spanish for the baths. It is named for pools known as the baths of Padre Arroyo, a regular stop for priests from Mission San Juan Bautista on trips into the valley, visited by Spanish explorers as early as 1805. Full story

  3. Exit 400 · Orchards & farms

    Tomato country

    The Morning Star Company, which opened its first tomato factory in Los Banos in 1990 and another at Santa Nella in 2002, was the world's leading tomato processor as of 2023. Full story

Los Banos and the Grasslands

Pumping plants, power lines and canals line the west side of the valley here, while the Grasslands spread along the San Joaquin River north and east of Los Banos.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Los Banos on I-5?

Los Banos is at exit 399 on I-5. The city itself is about 7 miles east of the freeway.

Why is Los Banos called Los Banos?

Los Banos is Spanish for the baths. It is named for pools known as the baths of Padre Arroyo, a regular stop for priests from Mission San Juan Bautista, and Spanish explorers visited as early as 1805.

Where is the nearest rest area near Los Banos on I-5?

Going north, Westley Rest Area is listed at mile 444.9, about 45.9 miles from here, with access in both directions. To the south, Dos Amigos Vista Point is listed at mile 390, about 9 miles from here; the data does not give its direction.
