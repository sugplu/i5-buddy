What to see near Downtown Los Angeles

  1. Exit 133 · Towns

    Downtown Los Angeles skyline

    The towers of downtown Los Angeles rise to the west, across the Los Angeles River. The one with a thin spire on top is the Wilshire Grand Center, finished in 2017.

  2. Exit 134 · on your left going north · History

    The Sears tower

    The tall Art Deco tower near the interchange belongs to the old Sears, Roebuck mail-order building, finished in 1927 in just 171 days.

  3. Exit 134 · on your left going north · Oddities

    East Los Angeles Interchange

    This tangle of ramps is the East Los Angeles Interchange, where Interstate 5, Interstate 10, U.S. 101 and State Route 60 meet on 135 acres of Boyle Heights.

  4. Exit 135 · History

    Mission San Gabriel

    Exit 135 is the ramp to Interstate 10 east, the way to Mission San Gabriel, about eight miles away in the city of San Gabriel. Full story

  5. Exit 135 · on your left going north · History

    Yaanga and the Tongva

    Across the river, where downtown Los Angeles now stands, was Yaanga, recorded as the largest and most influential Tongva village in the region. Full story

Downey, Commerce and East LA

The freeway crosses the San Gabriel River and the Rio Hondo and runs through aerospace Downey, the rail-side factories of Commerce, and East Los Angeles.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Downtown Los Angeles on I-5?

The Downtown Los Angeles skyline is noted at exit 133, Grande Vista Avenue. Other nearby exits include 135B for Cesar Chavez Avenue, 136B for Broadway and 136A for Main Street.

Where do I-5, I-10, US 101 and CA 60 meet near Downtown LA?

They meet at the East Los Angeles Interchange near exit 134, on the left going north, a tangle of ramps spread over 135 acres of Boyle Heights. Exit 134B is Soto Street and CA 60 East toward Pomona, and exit 135C is I-10 East toward San Bernardino.

Is there gas near Downtown Los Angeles on I-5?

Yes, gas stations are listed at many exits here, such as 3 at exit 133, 4 at exit 134A and 4 at exit 135C. This comes from OpenStreetMap as mapped, not live, so it does not show what is open or what it costs.

Where is the nearest rest area to Downtown Los Angeles on I-5?

Going south, the nearest is Aliso Creek Rest Area at mile 59.4, 73.6 miles from here. Going north, it is Tejon Pass Rest Area at mile 206.1, 73.1 miles from here, and both are open to both directions of travel.

What can I see from I-5 in Downtown Los Angeles?

Near exit 133 the towers of downtown rise to the west, including the Wilshire Grand Center with its thin spire, finished in 2017. Near exit 134 on the left going north is the old Sears, Roebuck mail-order tower, finished in 1927 in 171 days.
