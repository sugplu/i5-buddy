Redding on I-5 in California

Redding sits at the northwestern end of the Central Valley, with mountains on the north, east and west and farmland to the south. I-5 Buddy's main Redding story is at exit 678, where a spot once called Poverty Flats became a railroad town named for Benjamin Redding. The exits around town, from 675 to 681, have gas, EV charging, food and lodging as mapped in OpenStreetMap, not live.

What to see near Redding

  1. Exit 678 · on your left going north · Towns

    Redding, once Poverty Flats

    During the Gold Rush, miners found little gold in this ground, and the spot was called Poverty Flats. In 1868 the Central Pacific Railroad's land agent, Benjamin Redding, bought land here for a northern terminus, and the railroad built a town and named it for him. Full story

  2. Exit 675 · History

    The 1846 river massacre

    On April 5, 1846, an armed expedition led by Captain John C. Frémont attacked a large Wintu camp on the banks of the Sacramento River near what is now Redding. Full story

  3. Exit 677 · History

    The Carr Fire

    On July 26, 2018, the Carr Fire jumped the Sacramento River into the west side of Redding, and 38,000 people had to evacuate. Full story

  4. Exit 678 · on your left going north · Oddities

    Sundial Bridge

    West of the freeway, the Sundial Bridge, a 700-foot footbridge designed by Santiago Calatrava, crosses the Sacramento River. Full story

  5. Exit 680 · on your left going north · History

    Old Shasta

    Highway 299 leads to Shasta, about six miles west of Redding. Full story

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Redding on I-5?

The Redding landmark in I-5 Buddy is at exit 678, which serves CA 44 and Hilltop Drive toward Eureka and Susanville. Nearby exits run from 675 (Bechelli Lane) to 681B (CA 273, Market Street).

How did Redding get its name?

Miners found little gold here in the Gold Rush, and the spot was called Poverty Flats. In 1868 Central Pacific Railroad land agent Benjamin Redding bought land here for a northern terminus, and the railroad built a town and named it for him.

Where can I find gas and EV charging in Redding on I-5?

In the mapped data, exit 677 (Cypress Avenue) lists the most gas stations, 10, and exit 675 lists 5. EV charging is listed at exits 675, 677, 678, 678A and 678B, including Tesla Supercharger, ChargePoint and EVgo networks. This is from OpenStreetMap, not live.

Where can I find hotels and food in Redding off I-5?

Exit 678 lists 12 lodging places, including Holiday Inn, Best Western Plus, Comfort Inn and Hampton Inn & Suites. Exit 677 lists 47 food places and exit 678A lists 26, as mapped, not live.

Where is the Sundial Bridge near I-5 in Redding?

The Sundial Bridge is a 700-foot footbridge designed by Santiago Calatrava that crosses the Sacramento River. I-5 Buddy places it at exit 678, on the left going north, west of the freeway.
