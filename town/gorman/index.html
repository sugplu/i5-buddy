Gorman on I-5 in California

Gorman sits at exit 202 on I-5. In spring after a wet winter, poppies and lupine can cover the hills around town, and the spot was once the Tataviam village of Kulshra'jek, a trading crossroads. This page covers what is mapped at the Gorman exit and the next one north, plus landmarks and rest areas close by.

What to see near Gorman

  1. Exit 202 · Towns

    Gorman and its wildflowers

    In spring after a wet winter, California poppies and lupine can blanket the hills around Gorman. People have stopped here for centuries: it was the Tataviam village of Kulshra'jek, a trading crossroads, and a local historian calls it one of the oldest continuously used roadside rest stops in California. Full story

  2. Exit 204 · Land & water

    Tejon Pass summit

    Near the top of Tejon Pass, I-5 reaches 4,144 feet, its highest point in California, close to the line between Los Angeles and Kern counties. Full story

    The Brink's jewelry heist

    At 2 in the morning on July 11, 2022, a Brink's tractor-trailer carrying jewelry from a show in San Mateo to one in Pasadena pulled into a truck stop at the Frazier Mountain Park Road exit. Full story

  4. Exit 205 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Frazier Mountain

    The broad, pine-covered peak west of the pass is Frazier Mountain, 8,017 feet high. Full story

Pyramid Lake to Gorman

Pyramid Lake fills the canyon of Piru Creek west of the freeway in the Sierra Pelona Mountains, and farther north the land opens into Peace Valley around Gorman.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Gorman on I-5?

Gorman is at exit 202 on I-5. The next exits north are exit 204 and exit 205, Frazier Mountain Park Road.

Is there gas, food and lodging at the Gorman exit?

At exit 202 the data lists 5 gas stations (Shell, 76, Chevron and Gorman Chevron), 4 food places such as Carl's Jr., El Grullense, McDonald's and Ranch House, and 7 lodging places. This is as mapped in OpenStreetMap, not live, so check hours before you rely on a stop.

Where can I charge an EV near Gorman on I-5?

The mapped data shows no EV charger networks at exit 202. At exit 205, Frazier Mountain Park Road, ChargePoint and Tesla Supercharger are listed. This is as mapped, not live.

Where is the highest point of I-5 in California?

Near the top of Tejon Pass, around exit 204, I-5 reaches 4,144 feet, its highest point in California. It is close to the line between Los Angeles and Kern counties.

Where is the nearest rest area to Gorman on I-5?

The Tejon Pass Rest Area is 4.1 miles north of Gorman at mile 206.1 and serves both directions. South of town, the nearest listed is Aliso Creek Rest Area at mile 59.4, 142.6 miles back.
