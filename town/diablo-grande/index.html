Diablo Grande on I-5 in California

Diablo Grande is a gated hillside community in the Diablo Range, reached from I-5 at exit 434 by Diablo Grande Parkway. This page covers the exit, what is mapped there, and what you will pass on the way north.

Patterson and Westley

The Diablo Range rises close on the west, cut by Del Puerto Canyon, while Patterson, known for its distribution centers, sits east of the freeway, and on clear days Mount Diablo shows on the northwest skyline.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Diablo Grande on I-5?

Diablo Grande is at exit 434, signed Diablo Grande Parkway and Sperry Avenue. The parkway leads into the Diablo Range to the community, about 9 miles southwest of Patterson.

Is there gas, EV charging, food and lodging at exit 434?

The map data lists 8 gas stations at exit 434, including Love's, Chevron, National Petro and Valero. It also lists Tesla Supercharger and EVgo networks, 10 food places such as A&W, Arby's, Carl's Jr., Denny's and Dutch Bros. Coffee, and 2 lodging places, Best Hotels and Hampton. This is as mapped in OpenStreetMap, not live, so open hours and availability are not shown.

What is Diablo Grande?

Diablo Grande is a gated hillside community at 1,535 feet in the Diablo Range. Developer Donald Panoz dreamed of a resort town with up to 10,000 homes, six golf courses, a resort hotel and spa, vineyards and a winery.

Where is the nearest rest area to Diablo Grande on I-5?

Going north, Westley Rest Area is at mile 444.9, about 10.9 miles from here, and serves both directions. To the south, Dos Amigos Vista Point is at mile 390, about 44 miles away.
