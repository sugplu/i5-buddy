Alpaugh on I-5 in California

Take exit 305, Utica Avenue, and head east to reach Alpaugh, a town on what was once Atwell's Island, the largest island in Tulare Lake. It was first called Hog-Root Island, and a Visalia lawyer named Allen Atwell raised hogs there. North of the Kern County line, the old bed of Tulare Lake, now farmland, spreads east of the freeway.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Alpaugh on I-5?

Alpaugh is at exit 305, Utica Avenue. Follow Utica Avenue east from the freeway to reach the town.

Is there gas at the Alpaugh exit on I-5?

Two gas stations are mapped at exit 305, with Chevron and Shell listed. This comes from OpenStreetMap, so it shows what is mapped, not what is open now.

Where can I find food, EV charging and lodging near Alpaugh?

Exit 305 lists only gas. Four miles north, exit 309 (CA 41, Kettleman City) has mapped gas, 16 food places, 2 lodging places, and EV charging from Tesla Supercharger, EV Range and Electrify America. All of it is listed in the data, not live.

Where is the nearest rest area to Alpaugh on I-5?

The Coalinga-Avenal Rest Area is at mile 320.2, about 15.2 miles north of here, serving both directions. To the south, the Buttonwillow Rest Area is at mile 259.2, about 45.8 miles back.

What is the history of Alpaugh?

Alpaugh sits on what was once Atwell's Island, the largest island in Tulare Lake, first known as Hog-Root Island. A Visalia lawyer named Allen Atwell raised hogs on it.
