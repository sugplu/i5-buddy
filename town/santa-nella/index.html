Santa Nella on I-5 in California

Santa Nella is at exit 407 on I-5, where CA 33 meets the freeway. The name only sounds like a saint's: it is an English mangling of the Spanish centinela, from Rancho de Centinela, a horse ranch started here in 1810. The state and federal water projects also meet nearby, sharing San Luis Reservoir, its dam and its forebay.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Santa Nella on I-5?

Santa Nella is at exit 407, the CA 33 exit toward Santa Nella and Gustine.

Where does the name Santa Nella come from?

There is no Saint Nella. The name is an English mangling of the Spanish word centinela, meaning sentinel, from Rancho de Centinela, a horse ranch started here in 1810 by stockmen from San Juan Bautista and Monterey.

What services are at the Santa Nella exit on I-5?

The data maps 10 gas stations, 16 food places and 8 lodging places at exit 407, plus EV charger networks including Tesla Supercharger and bp pulse. These are as listed in OpenStreetMap, not live, so check before you rely on any one stop.

Is Pea Soup Andersen's in Santa Nella?

Yes, it is listed at mile 407.2, by exit 407. It opened there in 1976 as the second location of a roadside restaurant famous for its pea soup.

Where is the nearest rest area to Santa Nella on I-5?

To the south, Dos Amigos Vista Point is at mile 390, about 16.6 miles from Santa Nella. To the north, Westley Rest Area is at mile 444.9, about 38.3 miles away, and serves both directions.
