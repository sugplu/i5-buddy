Manteca on I-5 in California

Manteca sits at about mile 461 of I-5 in California, near exit 461. Highway 120 leads east to Manteca, whose name is Spanish for lard. That is an odd name for a town whose railroad stop was there to ship dairy products, and nobody agrees on how it happened. Below are the exits, services, landmarks and nearest rest areas around it.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Manteca on I-5?

Exit 461, signed CA 120 East; Manteca, Sonora. Highway 120 leads east from there to Manteca.

Is there gas near Manteca on I-5?

One gas station, an Arco, is listed at exit 460 in the map data. That is as mapped, not live, so it does not say whether it is open.

Where does I-5 cross the San Joaquin River near Manteca?

At Mossdale Crossing, near exit 460 on the left going north. The crossing is near a steel railroad lift bridge.

Why is Manteca named after lard?

Manteca is Spanish for lard, which is odd for a town whose railroad stop shipped dairy products. According to the I-5 Buddy landmark text, nobody agrees on how the name came about.
