What to see near Dunsmuir

  1. Exit 729 · Towns

    Dunsmuir, the railroad town

    Dunsmuir was a railroad town first, nicknamed Pusher, because extra steam engines were added here to push trains up the steep grades north of town. In 1888, Alexander Dunsmuir, son of a British Columbia coal baron, offered the town a fountain if it would take his name. Full story

  2. Exit 726 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Castle Crags

    These granite spires rise from about 2,000 feet at the river to more than 6,500 feet at the top. Full story

  3. Exit 728 · on your left going north · Good stops

    Sleep in a caboose

    Just off this exit, a lodge founded in 1968 lets guests sleep in old cabooses and eat in a restaurant inside an original train car, with Castle Crags towering above. Full story

  4. Exit 730 · History

    Upper Soda Springs

    Under the freeway in north Dunsmuir lies Upper Soda Springs, now a riverside park. Full story

  5. Exit 731 · Oddities

    Babe Ruth in Dunsmuir

    In 1924, New York Yankees stars Babe Ruth and Bob Meusel came to Dunsmuir on a barnstorming tour and played an exhibition game against the locals in the city park. Full story

  6. Exit 732 · Good stops

    Hedge Creek Falls

    A short trail from this exit leads to a waterfall with a small cave behind it, so you can walk behind the falling water. Full story

  7. Exit 733 · on your left going north · Oddities

    Where Shasta soda began

    Just north of Dunsmuir was Shasta Springs, a summer resort on the railroad main line in the late 1800s and early 1900s. Full story

Castle Crags and Dunsmuir

Granite spires rise west of the freeway, and an old railroad town fills the canyon bottom. Dunsmuir gets about 63 inches of rain and snow a year, about three and a half times as much as Yreka or Medford.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Dunsmuir on I-5?

Dunsmuir is at exit 729 (I 5 Business; Dunsmuir). Exit 730 (Central Dunsmuir) and exit 732 (Siskiyou Avenue, Dunsmuir Avenue) also serve the town.

Where is gas in Dunsmuir off I-5?

Gas is mapped at two exits: a Valero at exit 729 and a Chevron at exit 730. A ChargePoint EV charger is also mapped at exit 730. These are listed in the data, not live, so check before you rely on them.

Where can I eat in Dunsmuir near I-5?

Exit 730 (Central Dunsmuir) has the most food mapped, with seven places including Burger Barn, Cafe Maddalena and Pizza Factory. Exit 732 lists Penny's Diner and Sten's Burger Strand, and exit 728 lists the Jubilee Railroad Dining Car.

Where can I stay in Dunsmuir off I-5?

Exit 732 has the most lodging mapped, with eight listings including Cedar Lodge Motel and Travelodge. Exit 728 lists Jubilee Railroad Wilderness Lodge, where a lodge founded in 1968 lets guests sleep in old cabooses.

What is there to see in Dunsmuir on I-5?

Castle Crags is at exit 726, on the left going north. A short trail from exit 732 leads to Hedge Creek Falls, where you can walk behind the falling water, and Upper Soda Springs is a riverside park under the freeway at exit 730.
