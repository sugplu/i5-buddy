Carlsbad on I-5 in California

Carlsbad's main exit on I-5 is exit 50, Carlsbad Village Drive. The town is named for a spa town in Bohemia, and in the 1880s a former sailor named John Frazier dug a well here and offered its water at the train stop, which became known as Frazier's Station. This stretch of I-5 passes flower and strawberry fields and several lagoons before reaching Oceanside.

What to see near Carlsbad

  1. Exit 50 · Towns

    Carlsbad's mineral water

    Carlsbad is named for a spa town in Bohemia. In the 1880s a former sailor, John Frazier, dug a well here and offered its water at the train stop, which became known as Frazier's Station.

  2. Exit 47 · on your right going north · Orchards & farms

    The Flower Fields

    In spring, the hillside east of the freeway turns bright with flowers.

  3. Exit 48 · Orchards & farms

    Carlsbad Strawberry Company

    The strawberry fields beside the freeway at Cannon Road belong to the Carlsbad Strawberry Company, a pick-your-own farm run by third-generation farmer Jimmy Ukegawa.

  4. Exit 49 · on your right going north · Land & water

    Agua Hedionda and the desal plant

    The freeway crosses Agua Hedionda Lagoon, Spanish for fetid water.

  5. Exit 51 · on your left going north · Land & water

    Buena Vista Lagoon

    The freeway crosses Buena Vista Lagoon, the 223-acre border between Carlsbad and Oceanside and California's first ecological reserve.

Carlsbad and Oceanside

Carlsbad's flower and strawberry fields, three more lagoons, Batiquitos, Agua Hedionda and Buena Vista, and the beach town of Oceanside fill this stretch of Luiseño and Kumeyaay country.

Questions drivers ask

What exit is Carlsbad on I-5?

Carlsbad Village Drive is exit 50. Nearby exits are Palomar Airport Road (47), Cannon Road (48), Tamarack Avenue (49) and Las Flores Drive (51A).

Where can I find gas, food and lodging in Carlsbad on I-5?

Exit 50 at Carlsbad Village Drive lists 3 gas stations, 21 food places and 14 lodging places in the data. Exit 47 at Palomar Airport Road lists 5 gas stations, 30 food places and 8 lodging places. These come from OpenStreetMap as mapped, not live.

Where are the EV chargers near Carlsbad on I-5?

The data maps Tesla Supercharger and eVgo at exit 47, Electrify America at exit 48, EVgo at exit 49, and Electrify America and Tesla Supercharger at exit 51C. Exit 50 lists no EV charger networks. This is as mapped, not live.

What is the next rest area north of Carlsbad on I-5?

The Aliso Creek Rest Area, at mile 59.4, is the nearest one north of town, 9.4 miles from here, and serves both directions.

Why is it called Carlsbad?

Carlsbad is named for a spa town in Bohemia. John Frazier, a former sailor, dug a well here in the 1880s and offered its water at the train stop.
