Sponsor a stop

Your business, at your exit, in front of drivers on I-5.

I-5 Buddy is a free GPS road-trip guide for Interstate 5 in California, in English and Spanish. As people drive, it tells them what is coming up: towns, rest areas, places to stop and stories about the road. It is made by Dominares.

The offer

  • One stop at one exit: $49 a month. Month to month. Cancel any time by email.
  • Sponsored stops are not in the app yet. We are taking names for the first ones now, and we will confirm a start date with you before you pay anything.
  • You pay nothing until your stop is live in the app.
  • One sponsor per kind of business per exit. If you are the coffee stop at your exit, you are the only sponsored coffee stop there.

What drivers will see

  • Your name, what you offer and a short line you write, in the list of stops for your exit.
  • A clear Sponsored label. We never present a paid stop as our own pick.
  • A button that opens directions to your door.

Our rules

  • Sponsored stops will never be read aloud while someone is driving.
  • No tracking. We do not follow drivers or sell their data, so we will not be able to report who visited you.
  • Your business must be real, open, and close to an I-5 exit in California.
  • We may turn down a sponsor that does not fit a family road trip.

Who it fits

Restaurants, coffee, fuel and truck stops, fruit stands, motels, repair shops, and attractions a few minutes off the freeway.

How to start

Write to support@dominares.org with your business name, your I-5 exit and a phone number. We reply by email with the next steps.
